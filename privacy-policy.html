Introduction

Varahi Associates ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our insurance and financial services. Please read this policy carefully.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Personal Identification: Name, date of birth, gender, and government-issued ID details.
  • Contact Information: Phone number, email address, and residential/business address.
  • Financial Information: Income details, existing policies, and financial goals shared for advisory purposes.
  • Communication Data: Records of your enquiries, messages, and interactions with our team.
  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, and pages visited on our website (via cookies).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and personalise insurance and financial advisory services.
  • Process insurance applications and facilitate claims on your behalf.
  • Send you policy renewal reminders, service updates, and important notices.
  • Respond to your enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations under IRDAI guidelines.
  • Improve our website, products, and services based on usage data.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Insurance Partners: With our partner insurers (Tata AIG, Liberty General, IndusInd, Future Generali, Royal Sundaram, Digit, Chola MS, Oriental Insurance, Universal Sompo, United India) solely to process your insurance applications and claims.
  • Regulatory Bodies: With IRDAI or other regulatory authorities as required by law.
  • Legal Compliance: When disclosure is required by applicable law, court order, or government authority.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device. We use them to remember your preferences and analyse website traffic. You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, some features of the site may not function properly without them.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All sensitive data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal obligations).
  • Withdraw consent for marketing communications at any time.
  • Raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority.
